Company Boilerplate

Short (50 words)

School Linx is a cloud-based school management platform that gives educational institutions a fully integrated system for students, staff, grades, attendance, fees, and communications — all in one place. Trusted by 500+ schools across Africa and beyond.

Long (100 words)

Founded with a mission to modernize education administration across Africa, School Linx provides a comprehensive, multi-tenant SaaS platform for K-12 institutions. The platform covers the full spectrum of school management: student information, teacher management, academic grading, attendance tracking, fee collection, HR, and parent communication — all built on an enterprise-grade, isolated-database architecture that ensures absolute data security and privacy per school. School Linx serves 500+ institutions and is rapidly expanding across West Africa, East Africa, and internationally.
