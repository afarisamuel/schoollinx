Implementation

From zero to live in 48 hours.

Deploying a new school management system doesn't have to be a multi-month nightmare. Our dedicated team and streamlined process get you up and running before Monday.

Phase 1: Discovery & Provisioning

We meet with your administrative team to understand your specific needs, grading structures, and fee schedules. Simultaneously, our automated system provisions a secure, isolated database tenant exclusively for your school.

Phase 2: Data Migration

Send us your messy Excel spreadsheets, CSVs, or legacy database exports. Our data engineers will clean, format, and map all your existing student, staff, and financial records into School Linx.

Phase 3: Configuration

We set up your custom domain (e.g., portal.yourschool.edu), configure your Paystack/Stripe integration for fee collection, and establish your school's unique academic calendar and timetable.

Phase 4: Staff Training

We host interactive, role-specific training sessions via Zoom for your administrators and teachers. We provide comprehensive documentation and video tutorials for ongoing reference.

Phase 5: Go Live

You send out automated email and SMS invites to teachers, parents, and students. Your school officially enters the modern era of education management.
