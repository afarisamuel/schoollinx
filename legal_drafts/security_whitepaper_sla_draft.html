Security Whitepaper & SLA Guarantees

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 Version: 3.0
Zero-Trust Architecture: SchoolLinx ensures dedicated tenant isolation, continuous cryptographic verification, and sub-minute disaster recovery failover.

1. Multi-Tenant Relational Schema Isolation

Rather than relying on soft logical filters in a single shared database table, SchoolLinx provisions a distinct PostgreSQL schema for every enrolled school node. Cross-tenant leakage is strictly prevented at the database engine level.

2. Disaster Recovery & Availability Metrics

Performance Indicator Target SLA Metric Architecture & Protocol
Monthly Service Uptime 99.99% Multi-AZ redundant load balancers and container clusters.
Recovery Point Objective (RPO) < 5 Minutes Continuous PostgreSQL WAL archiving and geo-mirrored storage.
Recovery Time Objective (RTO) < 15 Minutes Automated standby failover and schema reconstruction scripts.
Daily Snapshot Backups Hourly + Daily Snapshots Encrypted geo-redundant storage replicated across 3 regions.

3. Incident Response & Support Tiers

Severity Level Definition Initial Response SLA
P1 - Critical Outage Complete school portal inaccessibility or inability to process attendance/billing. < 15 Minutes (24/7 dedicated incident response).
P2 - Major Feature Impact SMS carrier delivery delay or individual gradebook report rendering error. < 2 Hours during business hours.
P3 - Minor / Inquiries General configuration questions, onboarding queries, or report customization. < 24 Hours.

4. Cryptographic Encryption Standards

  • Disk & Volume Encryption: AES-256 with Customer-Managed Keys (CMEK).
  • Network Encryption: TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).
  • Biometrics: Unidirectional SHA-256 template hashing.
  • Verifiable Credentials: SHA-256 document hashing for tamper-proof graduation certificates.
