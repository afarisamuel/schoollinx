Governance & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Student Data Protection

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 Version: 2.4 Scope: Multi-Tenant Global Platforms
Core Commitment: SchoolLinx operates on a strict dedicated PostgreSQL schema isolation architecture. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest student or institutional data under any circumstance.

1. Definitions & Operational Architecture

In this Privacy Policy:

  • "Institution" or "School" refers to the educational entity operating an administrative node on SchoolLinx.
  • "Data Controller" refers to the educational institution that establishes student records, class enrollments, and staff permissions.
  • "Data Processor" refers to SchoolLinx Inc., which hosts and executes software services strictly on behalf of the Data Controller.
  • "Student Records" refers to any personally identifiable information, continuous assessments, biometric templates, and report cards tied to an enrolled pupil.

3. Biometric Data Protection & Irreversible Hashes

Where schools activate biometric attendance terminals or facial verification kiosks:

  • Raw fingerprint imagery or complete facial photographs are never transmitted to or stored in cloud databases.
  • The on-premise edge terminal converts physical points into a unidirectional cryptographic vector template (hash).
  • Biometric template hashes cannot be reverse-engineered to reconstruct physical fingerprints or imagery.
  • Upon student graduation or transfer, biometric template hashes are automatically purged from the tenant schema.

4. Cryptographic Security & Dedicated Schema Isolation

SchoolLinx enforces multi-layered enterprise defense:

  • PostgreSQL Schema Separation: Each educational institution operates in its own dedicated schema (e.g., tenant_accra_high). Cross-schema queries are blocked by database-level security barriers.
  • Encryption at Rest: All database storage volumes and automated snapshot backups utilize AES-256 cloud encryption with Customer-Managed Encryption Keys (CMEK).
  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP API, WebSocket, and portal traffic enforces TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Immutable Audit Logging: Every administrative action, grade alteration, fee adjustment, and data export is logged with actor email, IP address, and timestamp.

5. Verifiable Transcripts & Public Registry

When institutions publish digital transcripts or certificates with verifiable QR codes:

  • Only the public SHA-256 document hash and non-sensitive graduation status are verified against the public registry endpoint.
  • Unauthorized third parties cannot query full academic student files without the unique verified certificate token issued by the school.

6. Data Ownership, Portability & Right to Erasure

In accordance with GDPR (Articles 17 & 20) and national Data Protection regulations:

  • Full Data Ownership: The school retains exclusive ownership of all pupil and guardian data.
  • Automated Export: School Principals and SuperAdmins can export full institutional archives (JSON/CSV/PDF) on demand.
  • Permanent Sanitization: Upon verified institutional contract closure or formal deletion requests, isolated database schemas are purged using DoD 5220.22-M sanitization standards within 30 calendar days.
