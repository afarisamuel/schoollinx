Privacy & Tracking

Cookie & Tracking Technology Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 Version: 1.5
Zero Ad-Tracking Guarantee: SchoolLinx does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or data brokers. All cookies and storage keys are utilized exclusively to secure sessions, route database schemas, and store user preferences.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and browser LocalStorage are small key-value strings stored securely in your web browser. They ensure you stay securely logged in when navigating between gradebooks, fee ledgers, and attendance rosters.

2. Complete Inventory of Cookies and Storage Keys

Cookie / Storage Key Category Lifespan Purpose & Functionality
jwt_session / schoollinx_token Strictly Necessary Session / 24 Hours Encrypted JWT bearer token for role-based API authentication.
tenant_subdomain Strictly Necessary Persistent Identifies the school's isolated PostgreSQL database schema.
csrf_token Strictly Necessary Session Protects against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks on state-changing requests.
theme_preference / public-theme Preferences 1 Year Remembers whether you selected Light, Dark, or System visual appearance.
schoollinx_admin_sidebar_collapsed Preferences 1 Year Preserves your customized desktop sidebar layout state.
schoollinx_cookie_consent Preferences 1 Year Records your accepted cookie preferences and prevents repetitive prompts.
slx_telemetry_id Performance Telemetry 30 Days Anonymous session ID to measure page load latency and API error spikes without PII.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

Users can review and modify cookie permissions at any time via the Cookie Consent banner in the footer or by configuring their browser settings.
