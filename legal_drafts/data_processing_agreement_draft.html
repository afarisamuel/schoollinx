Compliance Addendum

Data Processing Addendum (GDPR / DPA)

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 Framework: GDPR (EU/UK) & National Data Protection Acts
Controller & Processor Relationship: Under this Addendum, the educational institution is the Data Controller, and SchoolLinx Inc. is the Data Processor. Processing occurs strictly under written instructions from the school.

1. Scope and Nature of Processing

The Processor shall process personal data exclusively for the following authorized educational management purposes:

  • Maintaining pupil enrollment records, attendance timestamps, and academic continuous assessments.
  • Executing automated tuition fee recovery and receipts reconciliation.
  • Transmitting SMS and email broadcasts to parents and staff upon school dispatch triggers.
  • Maintaining verifiable transcript hashes on behalf of the institution.

2. Authorized Sub-Processors

Sub-Processor Service Rendered Location & Safeguards
Cloud Infrastructure (AWS / Cloudflare) Encrypted database hosting, DDoS edge defense, SSL termination. EU / US ISO 27001 & SOC 2 certified datacenters.
Paystack Payments Tuition and wallet top-up payment gateway. PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateway.
Telecommunications Gateway Direct SMS carrier delivery and delivery receipts. Local carrier interconnects with TLS payload encryption.

3. Technical & Organizational Measures (TOMs)

  • Logical & Relational Schema Isolation: Hard segregation of database tables per tenant.
  • Customer-Managed Keys (CMEK): AES-256 field and disk-level cryptographic protection.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions restricting bursar, teacher, and registrar visibility.
  • 24/7 Security Incident Notification: Mandatory notification to the School within 48 hours in the event of a confirmed security breach.

4. Data Return & Deletion

Upon contract conclusion, the Processor shall, at the choice of the School, export all student records in structured JSON/CSV format and securely destroy all schema replicas in accordance with DoD 5220.22-M standards within 30 days.
